1. **ROBOT category has the tightest spreads of any category, with mean spreads 6.4–8.0 vs cross-category mean of ~11.** ROBOT_IRONING: 6.39, ROBOT_VACUUMING: 6.75, ROBOT_LAUNDRY: 7.17, ROBOT_DISHES: 7.35, ROBOT_MOPPING: 7.97 [cell 3]. All five are among the 6 tightest-spread products across the 50. Flag: `tight_spread` on all 5 ROBOT products [cell 23]. Tightest spread does NOT imply easier to trade — it means NPC quotes are already close. Needs corroboration: notebooks 03 (AR(1)), 08 (regime stability).

2. **SNACKPACK category has the widest spreads: mean 15.9–17.8.** SNACKPACK_STRAWBERRY is the widest product at 17.83; SNACKPACK_PISTACHIO the tightest in the group at 15.93 [cell 3]. SNACKPACK_STRAWBERRY has 10 distinct spread values; others have 7–8. The category has the lowest CV of any, indicating consistently wide but stable quoting (CV 0.096–0.099) [cell 3]. Flag: `wide_spread` on 4 of 5 SNACKPACK products [cell 23]. Cross-reference: notebook 07 (do flavor names encode spread level?), notebook 03 (do wide spreads coincide with mean-reversion?).

3. **OXYGEN_SHAKE_EVENING_BREATH is the only product with >90% of ticks at a single spread value: modal fraction = 93.4%** at modal spread = 12 [cell 14]. No other product exceeds 72%. This is a regime-anomaly standout. Notebook 09 (determinism/anomaly) should independently flag this. Needs corroboration: notebooks 01 (mode prevalence), 09 (hardcoded-FV check), 03 (AR(1) on this product).

4. **PEBBLES_XL is an outlier within its category: mean spread 16.6 vs PEBBLES_XS–PEBBLES_L range of 9.7–13.0.** PEBBLES_XL has 18 distinct spread values (highest in any category member) and a spread CV of 0.15 [cell 3, cell 19]. The four smaller PEBBLES have nearly identical imbalance (mean 0.00047, std 0.0720) but PEBBLES_XL shares that imbalance signature — suggesting the imbalance structure is category-wide despite the spread anomaly [cell 6]. Cross-reference: notebooks 05 (within-category correlation), 07 (size encodes price/spread?).

5. **All 50 products have L2 bid presence ≥ 73%; no product has L2 presence <50%.** 25 products have 100% L2 bid presence (all GALAXY_SOUNDS, all OXYGEN_SHAKE, all SNACKPACK, all UV_VISOR except AMBER, all PANEL except 1X4/2X2/4X4/1X2, PEBBLES_L/M/S/XL) [cell 8]. L2 side asymmetry (bid_l2_frac vs ask_l2_frac) is <0.01 for every product — no structurally missing side at L2 [cell 8]. L3 presence is 1–2% for all products (uniform, not informative) [cell 8].

6. **MICROCHIP products show the widest intra-category spread dispersion: 7.5 (OVAL) to 11.7 (SQUARE).** MICROCHIP_SQUARE has mean spread 11.7 and 12 distinct spread values vs MICROCHIP_OVAL at 7.5 with 9 distinct values [cell 3]. MICROCHIP_OVAL has the highest L1 imbalance of any product (mean 0.00249) and the highest CV spread (0.1921), suggesting more dynamic NPC quoting [cell 3, cell 6]. Cross-reference: notebook 03 (AR(1) on MICROCHIP — AGENT_BRIEF flags Microchips as different trade-count signature), notebook 09.

7. **L1 book imbalance is near-zero for all 50 products: |mean| max = 0.00302 (ROBOT_LAUNDRY).** No product shows a structurally biased L1 order book. All medians are exactly 0.0 [cell 6]. This means NPC bots do not persistently post more volume on one side at L1. Total depth asymmetry is similarly negligible — no product exceeds |0.001| in mean total depth asymmetry [cell 12]. Cross-reference: notebook 04 (signed flow proxy may reveal hidden directional pressure despite balanced book).

8. **No product has zero-net depth asymmetry in total depth; bid-ask depth ratios are all within 1% of 1.0.** PANEL category: bid/ask ratio = 1.0000; PEBBLES: 0.9998; ROBOT: ~0.9981 [cell 12]. The book is structurally symmetric in volume — no category has a measurably bid-heavy or ask-heavy posting structure [cell 12]. Cross-reference: notebook 04 (trade tape may break this symmetry), notebook 09 (look for hidden imbalance in second decimal places).

9. **ROBOT_IRONING and ROBOT_DISHES have anomalously high queue stickiness (both-price-unchanged fraction 40% and 27% respectively), while all other products are <10%.** ROBOT_IRONING: bid_stickiness 0.418, ask_stickiness 0.418, both = 0.404 [cell 10]. ROBOT_DISHES: 0.293/0.293/0.273. All other products: 0.01–0.22. This indicates ROBOT_IRONING's NPC bot holds its quote at the same price for an unusually long fraction of ticks. Cross-reference: notebooks 03 (AR(1) — sticky price = higher autocorrelation?), 08 (regime — is stickiness day-stable?).

10. **PEBBLES category has the lowest queue stickiness of all categories (both_stickiness mean = 0.015), meaning PEBBLES L1 prices move on nearly every tick.** PEBBLES_XL: 0.0076; PEBBLES_M: 0.0165; PEBBLES_S: 0.0181 [cell 10, cell 11]. This is consistent with the AGENT_BRIEF's observation that PEBBLES have a different trade-count signature (644 trades vs 733 for standard categories). Prices are highly dynamic. Cross-reference: notebook 03 (stationarity, AR(1)), notebook 09 (periodicity detection on PEBBLES), notebook 04 (high trade count + low stickiness).

11. **MICROCHIP_SQUARE and MICROCHIP_TRIANGLE also have very low stickiness (both 0.014–0.021), matching the PEBBLES/MICROCHIP special trade-signature categories in the AGENT_BRIEF.** MICROCHIP_SQUARE both_stickiness = 0.0142; MICROCHIP_TRIANGLE = 0.0205 [cell 10]. The AGENT_BRIEF notes Microchips at 569 trades vs 733 standard. These products' prices also move nearly every tick. Cross-reference: notebook 03 (FFT — do PEBBLES and MICROCHIP show periodicity?).

12. **Spread variability (CV) is lowest for SNACKPACK (0.096–0.099) and highest for PEBBLES_XS (0.200) and MICROCHIP_OVAL (0.192).** PEBBLES_XS and MICROCHIP_OVAL have the most unpredictable quote widths tick-to-tick [cell 3]. Products with high spread CV tend to also have lower modal spread fraction (<50%) [cell 14]. This co-occurrence flags them as more dynamically priced. Cross-reference: notebook 08 (vol clustering), notebook 09 (outlier detection).

13. **Spread regimes are NOT stable across days for 22 of 50 products (>10% range in mean spread day-over-day).** The 22 include all PEBBLES, all MICROCHIP, all ROBOTS, GALAXY_SOUNDS_BLACK_HOLES, GALAXY_SOUNDS_PLANETARY_RINGS, and OXYGEN_SHAKE_GARLIC [cell 4]. Only 3 products have <2% spread range across days: OXYGEN_SHAKE_EVENING_BREATH, SNACKPACK_RASPBERRY, SNACKPACK_VANILLA [cell 4]. Per the triage rubric, spread-stable products are better candidates for strategies that rely on consistent mid-price dynamics. Cross-reference: notebook 08 (day-over-day stability on all metrics).

14. **GALAXY_SOUNDS products all have identical imbalance statistics (mean −0.0002, std 0.1185) and identical depth asymmetry (0.0, std 0.008), suggesting their book structure is generated by the same NPC bot template.** All 5 GALAXY_SOUNDS products share these exact rounded values [cell 6, cell 12]. Same pattern holds for all OXYGEN_SHAKE, UV_VISOR, and SNACKPACK members within each category. Within-category book-structure cloning is a cross-category structural fact. Cross-reference: notebook 05 (within-category correlation), notebook 06 (clustering), notebook 09 (near-identical timeseries detection).

15. **Spread distribution is not Gaussian for any product — all show multi-modal or skewed patterns.** The modal spread fraction ranges from 24% (PEBBLES_XL, PEBBLES_XS) to 93% (OXYGEN_SHAKE_EVENING_BREATH) [cell 14]. Products with <50% at modal spread (25 products, including all PEBBLES, most MICROCHIP, most GALAXY_SOUNDS) have spread regimes where the mid-price is regularly shifting into a new pricing band, consistent with a trending or regime-switching underlying price [cell 14]. Cross-reference: notebook 01 (price distribution mode prevalence), notebook 03 (ADF stationarity), notebook 08 (regime detection).
